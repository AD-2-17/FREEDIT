Freedit page designs · v2

All 8 screens, in both themes. Use the switch on the right (or the sun / moon button inside any screen) to compare light and dark. In a small window the screens shrink to fit; they're designed for a laptop-width browser. Names and amounts are demo data; the wallet addresses are your real demo Editor and Client wallets, and the contract is the one live on Arc Testnet.

What changed from v1

  • Light and dark themes. The app follows your device setting, with a switch in the top bar.
  • One typeface (Geist), one accent (blue), black or white main buttons, line icons. No gradients, glows or blur.
  • Every project page shows a progress line and says in plain words what happens next.
  • Fees shown honestly: "network fee under $0.01" instead of a made-up total.
  • Demo numbers now add up (1 free revision, 1 paid at $15.00, then V3).

For you to decide

  • Headline. A (shown): "Get paid for every revision. Deliver the final cut when it's paid." B: "Freedit: paid revisions and pay-to-unlock delivery for video editors."
  • Accent colour. Blue is used only for links, timecodes and the current version. Keep it, or name another.
  • Approve with "approved", or tell me what to change (screen number + what).
