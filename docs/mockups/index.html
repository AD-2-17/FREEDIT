All 8 screens, in both themes. Use the switch on the right (or the sun / moon button inside any screen) to compare light and dark. In a small window the screens shrink to fit; they're designed for a laptop-width browser. Names and amounts are demo data; the wallet addresses are your real demo Editor and Client wallets, and the contract is the one live on Arc Testnet.

What changed

  • v3: the landing page (screen 1) is now an editorial, story-style page inspired by heldby, with Freedit's own colour, timeline graphic and copy. Screens 2–7 stay clean and compact for daily use.
  • Light and dark themes. The app follows your device setting, with a switch in the top bar.
  • One typeface (Geist), one accent (blue), black or white main buttons, line icons. No gradients, glows or blur.
  • Every project page shows a progress line and says in plain words what happens next.
  • Fees shown honestly: "network fee under $0.01" instead of a made-up total.
  • Demo numbers now add up (1 free revision, 1 paid at $15.00, then V3).

00:02The problem

Editors do "one last change" for free, again and again. Clients pay up front and get a file that isn't the one they approved. Nobody trusts the handoff.

Editors deliver the final file, then the client goes quiet. Revision seven was never part of the price.
Clients pay before seeing the final file, then can't tell whether it's really the version they signed off on.
00:03How it works

Four steps.
Nothing held.

01
Set the terms

Price, free revisions and the fee for each extra one. Share one link with your client.

02
Upload a version

A watermarked preview to watch, and the full file kept private. Its fingerprint goes on Arc.

03
Review and revise

Free revisions first. After that, each change request pays the editor on the spot.

04
Approve and unlock

The client pays the editor directly and the final file unlocks. It must match the fingerprint.

00:04The proof

The file you approved.
Byte for byte.

Every version's SHA-256 fingerprint is written to Arc when it's uploaded. After paying, your browser fingerprints the download and compares. Change one frame and it won't match.
